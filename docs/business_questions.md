# Business Questions

This project is designed to answer:

1. How many customers are currently active, and what are current MRR, ARR, and ARPA?
2. How have customer acquisition, churn, and MRR changed month by month?
3. What is the monthly logo churn rate?
4. How does retention evolve by signup cohort?
5. Which subscription plans have the highest observed churn share?
6. Is falling product usage associated with churn?
7. Is heavy support usage associated with churn?
8. Are recent failed payments associated with churn?
9. What does product usage look like in the months leading up to cancellation?
10. Which active accounts show multiple risk signals and may warrant customer-success review?

The project separates **descriptive association** from causality. It does not claim that any single factor causes churn.
